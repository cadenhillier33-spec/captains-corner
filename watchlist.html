Market Commentary

The Week Ahead

The market rolls into the week of March 16 with two overlapping drivers: a Fed decision on Wednesday and Nvidia's GTC event setting the tone for AI leadership. Retail sales on Monday will tell us whether the consumer is still carrying the tape, while Powell's press conference will decide whether yields calm down or re-tighten financial conditions. Our posture stays selective: favor durable secular themes with cash-flow visibility, keep semis tactical around GTC, and treat high-beta names like RKLB as seasoning, not the main course.
