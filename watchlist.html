Market Commentary

The Week Ahead

The market rolls into the week of March 30 with stress already elevated: the S&P 500 closed at 6,368.85, the 10-year finished near 4.44%, crude is back near $100, and the VIX is above 31. Tuesday brings JOLTS, Wednesday stacks ADP, retail sales, and ISM manufacturing into one morning, Thursday delivers February trade data, and Friday’s jobs report is the main event. Our posture stays selective: favor businesses with real cash flow and durable demand, treat AI leaders like quality but not immunity, and keep speculative exposure like RKLB sized like speculation.

Sectors on Watch

⚡ Power / Utilities: Still one of the cleaner themes on the board because AI infrastructure needs actual electricity, not just PowerPoint slides. VST remains a strong expression of that setup.

🛡️ Defense / Government Tech: Geopolitical stress plus sticky public-sector software demand keep mission-critical platforms like PLTR in focus.

🤖 AI Infrastructure: Long-term demand is excellent, but semis need rates and sentiment to cooperate. Great businesses can still have lousy entry points for a week or two.

🔐 Cybersecurity: Durable end-market, weaker tape. We still like the category, but leadership needs to reassert itself before we get cute with position size.

🚀 Space / Defense Innovation: Attractive long-term optionality, very little mercy from the market in the short term. Small size only until momentum improves.
