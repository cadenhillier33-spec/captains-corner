Market Commentary

The Week Ahead

The week of April 13 starts with a cleaner setup than last Sunday, but not a relaxed one. CPI has already hit, so the market now shifts from inflation shock to confirmation: can big-bank earnings, bond behavior, and oil headlines support the rebound, or does the rally run out of runway? Tuesday’s bank reports from JPMorgan, Wells Fargo, Citigroup, and BlackRock should give us the first real read on credit quality, deal activity, and management tone. Wednesday’s retail sales release was officially pushed to April 21 by the Census Bureau, so the near-term macro focus shifts back toward rates, Fed speakers, and earnings. Our posture stays the same: keep size biggest in quality compounders and infrastructure names, and keep anything story-heavy like RKLB sized like speculation, not religion.

Sectors on Watch

⚡ Power / Utilities: Still a leadership group because AI demand requires actual generation capacity. VST remains one of the best public-market ways to express that thesis.

🛡️ Defense / Government Tech: Pentagon adoption and geopolitical noise keep this bucket relevant, but headline risk is rising alongside valuations. PLTR stays in focus for that reason.

🤖 AI Infrastructure: Demand is still massive, but the group remains sensitive to yields and any hint that spending normalizes. Great businesses, less forgiving tape.

🔐 Cybersecurity: Secular demand remains healthy, and AI-native security work is becoming more valuable, not less. We want the category — just with respect for recent volatility.

🚀 Space / Defense Innovation: Better narrative momentum this week, but still a speculation bucket. Attractive optionality, lousy place for oversized conviction.
