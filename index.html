🌅 The Horizon Scan

The market enters May with momentum, but not much margin for error: the S&P 500 closed near 7,230, Nasdaq leadership remains intact, and the VIX has cooled to roughly 17. That is a healthy tape — as long as earnings and labor data do not reopen the inflation/rates problem.

This week shifts from Fed week to proof week. Palantir, AMD, Disney, Uber, Vistra, Rocket Lab, and Coinbase all report, giving investors fresh reads on AI software, chips, consumer demand, power demand, space, and crypto beta.

Friday is the macro pressure point. The BLS Employment Situation report is the major release, with payrolls, unemployment, participation, and wage growth all capable of moving Treasury yields. FMP’s calendar also flags Michigan Consumer Sentiment and inflation expectations later Friday.

Why this week matters: the bull case is simple — earnings keep broadening while labor data cools enough to protect the rate-cut narrative. The bear case is equally simple: hot wages or weak guidance pushes investors back into “great company, wrong price” mode.

🎪 What If?

Scenario 1, AI software earns the multiple: If Palantir delivers clean growth and confident guidance Monday, the market gets another reason to keep paying for operational AI winners.

Scenario 2, semis split the tape: AMD and Super Micro report Tuesday. Strong AI demand can help the whole complex, but any margin or supply-chain disappointment will hit the higher-multiple names first.

Scenario 3, power demand stays investable: Vistra’s Thursday report is a direct test of the data-center electricity thesis after a sharp pullback. Good numbers can stabilize the group fast.

Scenario 4, payrolls re-price rates: A hot jobs/wage print Friday likely pushes yields higher and pressures long-duration growth. A cooler-but-not-cracking report is the cleanest outcome for risk assets.

🗓️ The Week Ahead

Monday: Palantir reports after the close. For this watchlist, it is the most important single-stock event of the week because AI software needs numbers to keep outrunning valuation concerns.

Tuesday: AMD, Shopify, Super Micro, and MicroStrategy report. Semis and AI infrastructure get another demand check, while ISM Services and trade data help frame the growth backdrop.

Wednesday: Disney and Uber report, giving the market a consumer/services read. Fed speakers stay active, so watch whether officials lean against the easing in financial conditions.

Thursday: Vistra, Rocket Lab, Coinbase, Novo Nordisk, productivity, unit labor costs, and jobless claims hit the tape. For the watchlist, VST and RKLB are the big catalysts.

Friday: The BLS Employment Situation report is the week’s macro anchor, with payrolls, unemployment, and wage growth all in focus. Michigan Consumer Sentiment and inflation expectations add a second inflation-sentiment check later in the morning.

Simple takeaway: this is an earnings-and-jobs week. The clean outcome is solid company guidance plus labor data that cools enough to protect rates without signaling a demand break.

⚔️ Strategic Notes

Risk Management: Stay constructive, but do not let a calm VIX talk you into loose standards. Single-name earnings and Friday payrolls can move this tape quickly.

Positioning: Favor AI software, semis with demand confirmation, power/data-center beneficiaries, cybersecurity, and high-quality growth. Avoid low-quality beta until rates stop threatening the long-duration trade.

Macro Lens: The 10-year near 4.40% keeps the labor-data conversation alive. Markets can handle firm rates if earnings are strong; they struggle when firm rates meet wage pressure or soft guidance.

Portfolio Posture: Respect momentum, keep cash optionality, and use PLTR/VST/RKLB plus payrolls as the signal for whether to press risk or tighten up.
