🌅 The Horizon Scan

From Bad to Worse: February closed as the worst month for equities since the COVID crash. The S&P 500 shed 5.4%, the Nasdaq plunged 8.7%, and the VIX spiked above 23 — signaling genuine fear, not just volatility. The catalyst was a toxic cocktail: hotter-than-expected inflation data, a potential Trump trade war, and an AI bubble that finally showed cracks.

The PPI Shock: Friday's Producer Price Index report delivered the knockout punch. Core PPI surged 0.8% month-over-month — nearly triple the 0.3% consensus. This wasn't a rounding error; it was a wake-up call. Markets immediately repriced Fed cut expectations, with the first fully-priced cut now pushed to September. The 10-year Treasury yield climbed toward 4.3%, pressuring growth stocks already reeling from stretched valuations.

Gold's Parabolic Run: While tech burned, gold soared. The yellow metal broke $2,850 and is trading at all-time highs as investors flee to safety. The gold-to-S&P ratio is flashing warning signs — this is classic risk-off behavior. The dollar index recovered to 98.4, but remains vulnerable to escalating trade tensions.

Crypto Cracks: Bitcoin failed to hold $70K and is now testing $65K support. The crypto trade has become increasingly correlated with tech stocks — when the Nasdaq sneezes, Bitcoin catches pneumonia. With risk appetite evaporating, further downside is likely unless Powell rides to the rescue this week.

🎪 What If?

Scenario 1 — ISM Services Surprise: Tuesday's ISM Services PMI (consensus 52.5) could surprise to the upside if January's weather distortion fades. Above 53 = relief rally in cyclicals and small-caps. Below 50 = recession fears return, utilities and staples outperform.

Scenario 2 — Powell's Congressional Testimony: Wednesday and Thursday, Powell faces Congress with hot PPI fresh in mind. If he pushes back on March rate-cut expectations (our base case), growth stocks face another leg down. If he sounds dovish, the relief rally could be violent — but we doubt it.

Scenario 3 — Jobs Report Shocker: Friday's Nonfarm Payrolls (consensus 185K) could reignite inflation fears if it prints above 220K. Watch the wage component closely — 0.4%+ month-over-month would signal sticky inflation and crush rate-cut hopes. Financials benefit, tech gets hammered.

Scenario 4 — Tariff Tantrum: Trump's tariff threats remain real. If new levies are announced mid-week, expect immediate volatility. Defense contractors and domestic manufacturers could see rotation interest.
