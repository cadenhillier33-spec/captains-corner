📊 Week in Review

Inflation Shock and AI Anxiety: Friday's hot PPI report sent markets tumbling into the weekend. Headline PPI jumped 0.5% (vs 0.3% expected) while Core PPI surged 0.8% — more than double the 0.3% forecast. This was the inflation surprise that broke the camel's back after a week of AI bubble concerns.

The Nvidia Paradox: Wednesday's NVDA earnings were stellar — record data center revenue, strong guidance — yet the stock still fell 6.7% for the week. When great results aren't enough, it signals valuation concerns and profit-taking in the AI trade. The Mag 7 collectively dropped 6%, dragging broader markets lower.

Gold's Safe Haven Rally: While tech crumbled, gold surged 3.35% to $5,281 as investors fled to safety. The fear trade was on — CoreWeave crashed 18% on weak guidance, Dell soared 21% on AI server demand, and the VIX spiked 8.4% Friday alone.

The Bottom Line: February ended with all major indexes negative for the month. Markets are repricing for "higher for longer" rates as sticky inflation data suggests the Fed may not cut until late 2026. Monday brings new data — but Friday's selloff changed the narrative.

🎪 What If?

Scenario 1 — ISM Services Surprise: Tuesday's ISM Services PMI (consensus 52.5) could surprise to the upside if January's weather distortion fades. Above 53 = relief rally in cyclicals and small-caps. Below 50 = recession fears return, utilities and staples outperform.

Scenario 2 — Powell's Congressional Testimony: Wednesday and Thursday, Powell faces Congress with hot PPI fresh in mind. If he pushes back on March rate-cut expectations (our base case), growth stocks face another leg down. If he sounds dovish, the relief rally could be violent — but we doubt it.

Scenario 3 — Jobs Report Shocker: Friday's Nonfarm Payrolls (consensus 185K) could reignite inflation fears if it prints above 220K. Watch the wage component closely — 0.4%+ month-over-month would signal sticky inflation and crush rate-cut hopes. Financials benefit, tech gets hammered.

Scenario 4 — Tariff Tantrum: Trump's tariff threats remain real. If new levies are announced mid-week, expect immediate volatility. Defense contractors and domestic manufacturers could see rotation interest.
